Our Philosophy

Open-source hardware has the potential to accelerate innovation, strengthen supply chains, reduce barriers to entrepreneurship, and improve access to technology worldwide.

Open Hardware Ventures exists to build sustainable businesses that support these ecosystems while delivering meaningful products and services to researchers, educators, industry, and society.
